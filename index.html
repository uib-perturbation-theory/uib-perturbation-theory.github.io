We listen to the ringing of spacetime.

We study black holes, gravitational waves and strong-field gravity — from the mathematics of perturbed spacetimes to the signals measured by LIGO, Virgo and the detectors of the next decade.

What we do

Black holes as laboratories for fundamental physics

01 · Ringdown

Black hole spectroscopy

How quasinormal modes are excited: the ringdown as a spectral filter of the initial perturbation, the suppression of late-time tails, and ringdown tests of general relativity.

02 · Environments

Black holes in astrophysical environments

The imprint of gas and dark-matter clouds on extreme-mass-ratio inspirals observed by LISA, from gravitational atoms to the Little Red Dots.

03 · Neutron stars

Neutron stars and relativistic fluids

Oscillation modes of viscous neutron stars in a causal and stable theory of relativistic hydrodynamics, and new viscosity-driven modes.

04 · Methods

Numerical methods

Time-domain perturbation solvers, quasinormal-mode computations, numerical relativity, gravitational-wave data analysis and physics-informed neural networks.
